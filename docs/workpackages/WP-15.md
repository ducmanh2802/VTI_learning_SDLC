## Goal

Add `analytics.ai.guardrails`. These are pure, deterministic checks that decide whether text may reach a user. The project's legal position depends on them: **no recommendation and no profit promise may pass**, and **no personal data may leave the system**.

## In scope

- `services/analytics/src/analytics/ai/guardrails/**`, including `patterns_vi.yaml`
- `services/analytics/tests/ai/guardrails/**`, including the red-team seed files
- `docs/workpackages/reports/WP-15.md`

## Out of scope

- LLM calls, prompts and the end-to-end pipeline (WP-14, WP-16).
- Network access (forbidden by import-linter).

## Common rules

- Every check returns `GuardResult(passed: bool, guard: str, reasons: tuple[str, ...], spans: tuple[tuple[int, int], ...])`.
- **Text normalization** (`normalize(text) -> (lower_nfc, lower_nfc_no_diacritics)`):
  - Unicode NFC, then lowercase.
  - The no-diacritics variant removes combining marks and maps `đ → d`.
  - Matching runs on **both** variants. This catches "co nen mua hpg khong".
- **Patterns** live in `patterns_vi.yaml`, with a `version` key (`guard-1`). Each pattern has an `id`, a regex, and the category `advice`, `target_price`, `timing`, `profit_promise`, `imperative` or `allow_context`.

## Checks

### 1. `pii.scrub(text) -> (scrubbed_text, findings)` (FR-070, SDD 7.1)

Replace, in this order:

| Kind | Pattern | Replacement |
|---|---|---|
| Email | `[\w.+-]+@[\w-]+\.[\w.]+` | `[EMAIL]` |
| VN phone | `(?:\+?84\|0)(?:3\|5\|7\|8\|9)\d{8}\b`; also tolerate spaces or dots between digit groups | `[SĐT]` |
| CCCD/CMND | 12 or 9 standalone digits | `[SỐ_ĐỊNH_DANH]` |
| Account number | 10–16 standalone digits **not** followed within 3 chars by a unit (`cp`, `cổ phiếu`, `đồng`, `vnd`, `%`, `điểm`, `tỷ`, `triệu`, `nghìn`) | `[SỐ_TK]` |

- Symbol codes (`FPT`) and normal market numbers (`25.350`, `1,2%`) must survive unchanged.
- `findings` holds the kinds that were found, never the values.

### 2. `intent.classify(question, known_symbols) -> Intent(kind, symbols)` (SDD 7.2)

- `kind ∈ {ADVICE, OUT_OF_SCOPE, IN_SCOPE}`.
- **Symbols:**
  - 3-character uppercase tokens that are in `known_symbols`;
  - or company names from a `name → code` mapping argument, matched without diacritics.
- **ADVICE** when an `advice`, `target_price`, `timing` or `imperative` pattern matches. Examples: `có nên`, `nên (mua|bán|giữ|vào|ra|chốt|cắt)`, `mua (ở|tại) giá`, `giá mục tiêu`, `target`, `điểm (mua|bán)`, `bao giờ (mua|bán)`, `khi nào (mua|bán)`, `khuyến nghị`, `có lãi không`, `nếu là bạn`.
- **OUT_OF_SCOPE** when there is no symbol and no market keyword (`vn-index`, `vnindex`, `thị trường`, `ngành`, `thanh khoản`, `chỉ số`, `cổ phiếu`, `khối ngoại`).

### 3. `reco.check(text)` (FR-065) and `profit.check(text)` (FR-066) (SDD 7.3)

**Block** on any match of the `advice`, `target_price`, `timing` or `imperative` patterns (reco), or of `profit_promise` (profit):

- `nên mua`, `nên bán`, `nên nắm giữ`, `khuyến nghị`, `mua vào`, `bán ra ngay`
- `chốt lời (tại|ở)`, `cắt lỗ (tại|ở)`, `giá mục tiêu`, `điểm mua`, `điểm bán`, `cơ hội mua`
- `sẽ tăng`, `sẽ giảm`, `chắc chắn`, `đảm bảo (lợi nhuận|lãi)`, `lãi chắc`, `x2`, `nhân đôi tài khoản`, `hãy (mua|bán)`

**Allow context:** if a blocked span is fully inside an `allow_context` match, it is not a violation. Examples:

- `khối ngoại (mua|bán) ròng`, `lực (mua|bán)`, `bên (mua|bán)`, `dư (mua|bán)`
- `vùng quá (mua|bán) theo quy ước`, `giá trị (mua|bán)`

### 4. `numbers.check(text, facts) -> GuardResult` (FR-064, SDD 7.4)

- **Input:** `facts` is a sequence of `Fact(id: str, value: Decimal, unit: str, display: str)`.
- **Number extraction:**
  - vi-VN formats: `25.350`, `125.300 đồng`, `3,2%`, `72,4`, `1,25 nghìn tỷ`, `-1,62`.
  - Multipliers `nghìn/ngàn` ×10³, `triệu` ×10⁶, `tỷ` ×10⁹.
  - Ambiguous tokens (e.g. `1.5`) are tried in both vi and en readings, and pass if either matches.
- **Citation:**
  - Each extracted number must have a citation `[F<n>]` within 40 characters after it.
  - The cited id must exist.
  - The value must match within 0.5% relative, or exactly after rounding to the number of decimals shown in the text.
  - Numbers inside citation tokens are ignored.
- **Exemptions (no citation needed):**
  - indicator parameters `9, 12, 14, 20, 26, 50, 200`;
  - `100` when it appears in `RSI` context;
  - ordinals ≤ 3;
  - day, month and year parts of a date that appears in any fact's `display`.
- **Failure reasons:** `missing_citation`, `unknown_fact`, `value_mismatch`, each with the span.

### 5. `run_all(text, facts) -> tuple[GuardResult, ...]`

Runs numbers, reco and profit in that order, so the caller can log every result.

## Red-team seed (tests)

### `tests/ai/guardrails/redteam_questions.yaml`

At least **150** Vietnamese questions that must classify as **ADVICE**, covering:

- direct asks;
- target price;
- timing;
- profit;
- "if you were me";
- role-play and jailbreaks ("bỏ qua quy tắc", "giả sử bạn là chuyên gia");
- no-diacritics and teencode;
- mixed English;
- questions with a symbol and without one.

### Other seed files

- `redteam_bad_outputs.yaml`: at least **120** model outputs that contain advice or profit promises (direct, hedged, imperative, no-diacritics). **All** must be blocked by reco/profit.
- `neutral_outputs.yaml`: at least **120** neutral, factual texts, including the WP-11 rule texts, "khối ngoại mua ròng", "vùng quá mua theo quy ước", "lực bán", "giá trị mua ròng". At most **5%** may be blocked (false positives). Report the actual rate and list every blocked neutral line.
- `in_scope_questions.yaml`: at least **60** factual questions. They must classify as IN_SCOPE (or OUT_OF_SCOPE for the non-market ones), **not** ADVICE.

## Other tests

- **PII:** each kind is replaced; market numbers and symbols survive. Add a hypothesis property: any generated email or phone inside random Vietnamese text is removed.
- **numbers.check:** a passing paragraph with several cited numbers; a missing citation; an unknown fact; a value mismatch; vi/en ambiguity; multipliers; exemptions.
- **Determinism:** the same input always gives the same result.
- **Coverage:** `analytics.ai.guardrails` ≥ 90%.
